iPhone.

Everything in the table, plus:

Live Activity
Now Playing in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and the TV sign-in while it happens.
Widgets
Now Playing and Recently Played, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.
Controls
Play or Pause, Resume and Identify Song in Control Center, or on the Action button.
Wake to This
An alarm that wakes you with a track, playlist or station.
Match My Workout
Your heart rate from Apple Watch sets how hard Auto Mix goes.
Signs in the Apple TV
Listens for the TV’s chime, or scans its QR code.
Share to the Apple TV
Share a SoundCloud link and pick Play Now, Play Next or Add to End.
Volume buttons
With Sounding open, they turn the TV up and down.
Scout
A notification when an artist you scout posts a new track or set.
Spotlight
Finds your tracks, playlists and artists.

iPad.

Same app as the iPhone, laid out for the bigger screen.

The bar along the bottom
Shuffle, transport, repeat, Lyrics and Up Next, like Music on iPad.
Sidebar
Home, Search and Decks, then your Library and every playlist.
Now Playing in two columns
The player on the left; comments, lyrics or Up Next on the right.
Drag and drop
Tracks, albums and playlists into Up Next or onto a playlist.
Keyboard
Space to play, ⌘L to like, ⌘1 to ⌘9 to get around.
More windows
Open an artist or a playlist in a new window.
And most of the iPhone list
Widgets, controls, Wake to This, Scout, Spotlight and Share to the Apple TV.

Mac.

The DJ side lives here. It’s all on the DJ side of the homepage.

Decks
Two decks and a mixer: EQ, filter, sync, key lock, Audio Unit effects, cue in your headphones.
Prep panel
On any track (⌘I or ⌥⌘P), or on the deck: beat grids, keys, hot cues A to H, memory cues and loops.
rekordbox in and out
Import a rekordbox.xml, folder or USB stick. Export a new XML with your prep.
Export to USB
A stick the CDJ-2000NXS2 and CDJ-3000 can read (⇧⌘E). Not tried on a real CDJ yet.
DDJ-400
Mapped, jogs to pads. Controllers play your own music only.
DJ Players
Sees CDJs on the network, writes your setlist, joins as a virtual deck. Only met a fake CDJ so far.

Mac, the rest of it.

Menu bar player
Play, skip and see what’s on without opening the window.
Now Playing window
Full window, or the player in the toolbar (⇧⌘N).
Menus and shortcuts
Playback, Library, Decks and the Prep panel all have them.
Mac-only sidebar
Your Tracks, Private Shares and Messages.
Share extension
Share a SoundCloud link to Sounding: Play Now, Play Next or Play Last.
Heavy lifting
The Mac works out tempo and key, and your iPhone and iPad pick the results up.
Deeper analysis
On-device AI analysis, off until you turn it on.

Apple TV.

The loud one. More on the TV page.

Signs in by ear
Your iPhone hears its chime and signs it in. QR code if the room’s loud.
Now Playing
The artwork as the background, laid out like a tape deck, the next two tracks beside it.
Auto Mix and Decks
Watch the mix happen, and a Decks tab for the room.
Top Shelf
Continue Listening, Recently Played, Your Likes and Mixes For You. Select one and it plays.
Lyrics, full screen
Over the artwork, off until you say yes.
Ambient
Leave it alone and it’s just the artwork, big.
Output
Send it on to AirPlay or Bluetooth speakers, or a Sonos.

Apple Watch.

A remote for your iPhone, and for the TV when that’s playing.

A remote with a crown
Play and skip. The crown sets the volume when the TV’s playing, and scrubs when the iPhone is.
Output
The iPhone or an Apple TV, with a volume slider.
Up Next
The iPhone’s real queue. Tap a track to jump to it.
Auto Mix
Turn it on, Mix Now, from your wrist.
Match My Workout
Start a workout and Auto Mix follows your heart rate.
Identify Song
Listens on the Watch’s own microphone.
Complication
Now Playing on the watch face and in the Smart Stack, with play and pause.
Siri
Play, Pause, Next Track, Play Likes, Shuffle Likes and Like Track.

Widgets, extensions and notifications.

Widgets
Now Playing and Recently Played, small, medium and Lock Screen. iPhone and iPad.
Live Activity
Now Playing and TV sign-in. iPhone.
Controls
Play or Pause, Resume and Identify Song. iPhone and iPad; Play or Pause on the Watch.
Complication
Now Playing on the watch face. Apple Watch.
Top Shelf
Four shelves of what you’re listening to. Apple TV.
Share extension
Send a SoundCloud link to the Apple TV from an iPhone or iPad, or to Sounding on the Mac.
Notifications
An Apple TV asking to sign in, a new sign-in on your account, and Scout. iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Privacy.

All on device. The privacy policy has the detail.

No Sounding servers
No Sounding account, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, no ads of mine.
Your sign-in stays yours
In the Keychain and your private iCloud. It only ever goes to SoundCloud.
Sync through your own iCloud
Cue points, settings, Recently Played and analysis. I can’t read any of it.
Off until you say
Lyrics, heart rate, home lights and the Mac’s AI models.
See for yourself
Settings, Network Activity, lists every request the app makes.

Fair warning.

It’s a beta, made by one person in the evenings. Some of this will be rough round the edges, and some of it will change under you.
