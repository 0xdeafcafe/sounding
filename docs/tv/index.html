Turn it up.

Sounding on Apple TV is your SoundCloud and your own music on the big screen. Sign it in from your iPhone, iPad or Mac without touching a keyboard, move a set onto it mid-track, and run the lot from whatever’s in your hand.

Invite only. I made this for me, and for a few people who now owe me a favour.

Signed in by ear.

Open Sounding on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. A card pops up with the TV’s name on it. Tap Sign In and the TV plays a short sound with a signal in it too high for you to hear. Your device hears it, and the TV’s signed in. Or scan the code. Either way, no typing a password with the Siri Remote.

Linking something new? Six digits. The first time an iPhone, iPad or Mac links to a TV that’s already signed in, the TV shows a code and asks. Check it’s the same code on your device and press Allow. That goes for your own Apple ID too, so a neighbour on your Wi-Fi can’t just help themselves.

After that, it just reconnects. No code, no card. Open Sounding and it’s linked again, and the TV tells you who just turned up.

Someone else’s account on the TV? Your iPhone asks whether to play on theirs or switch the TV to yours, and the TV has to say yes as well. Nobody gets signed out from the next room without someone in the room agreeing.

Linked to Living Room

Mid-track, onto the telly.

Tap Play On and pick the Apple TV. Whatever’s on your iPhone carries on from the same second on the TV, just louder.

Already playing? Then it’s a remote. If the TV’s got something on, Play On takes the controls instead of talking over it. And if the TV starts playing while your phone’s been sat there idle, your phone follows it.

Your iPhone, iPad and Watch are the remote. Skip, pause, shuffle, sort out Up Next, and turn it up with the volume buttons or the Digital Crown. That’s Sounding’s own volume on the TV, so the telly’s level stays where you left it.

Every Now Playing bar knows. Pick up the iPad and it says Living Room is playing, with Control, Play On and Play Here. The Mac and the Watch show it too.

Made for the big one.

This is the Top Shelf. Put Sounding in the top row and the shelf fills with Continue Listening, Recently Played, your Likes and Mixes For You. Pick one, or just press Play, and it starts. Continue Listening picks up where you stopped.

This is Now Playing. The artwork blurs into the background and line art moves with the music. Swipe or click along the waveform to jump 15 seconds at a time, and click to play from there. Leave it alone and it goes ambient, with Auto Mix telling you what’s coming.

Lyrics, full screen. Synced line by line, from LRCLIB, once you’ve said yes to it. Press Lyrics and they take over the whole telly, which is either exactly what the party needed or a terrible idea.

SharePlay, from the sofa. On a FaceTime call, start SharePlay from More and everyone listens along on their own devices, in sync, each with their own SoundCloud sign-in. Auto Mix sits it out while you’re sharing.

Decks from the sofa.

The Apple TV has decks of its own, and your iPhone or iPad can drive them.

Mixing on Living Room. Open Decks on your iPhone or iPad and it runs the TV’s decks: cue, play, sync, tempo, EQ, filter and the crossfader, plus Mix Now, Load Next and Extend. The sound comes out of the TV. Hand Back when you’re done.

Or just use the Siri Remote. Swipe across a deck to sweep its filter, click to play or mix now, and hold for a loop. Leave it alone for 15 seconds and Decks goes into show mode, which looks quite good on a big screen.

Channel faders, loops and hot cues aren’t on the phone remote yet.

The small stuff.

The bits that make it feel like it belongs on the telly.

One search. On the TV, Search looks through your library and SoundCloud at once, Top Result first, so you only fight the on-screen keyboard once.

Your own music, from across the house. The TV plays the folders on your Mac, iPhone or iPad over your home network while Sounding’s open there, and from iCloud if you turn on Upload to iCloud. No SoundCloud account needed for that bit.

Carry on from your phone. Home offers whatever your iPhone, iPad or Mac was just playing, from where it stopped. It works the other way too: Handoff picks the TV’s track up on your phone.

Play On, from the TV. The TV’s own Output lists your Sonos rooms, plus AirPlay and Bluetooth.

Lights that follow the music. With Home Lighting on, the TV sets the HomeKit lights you pick while it’s the Output. Subtle or Party.

Auto Mix, the full set. Your Moves and Follow the Night are in the TV’s Settings too, and Match My Workout on your iPhone sends your Watch’s heart rate over when the TV’s playing. BPM and key come from your iPhone, iPad or Mac.

Fair warning.

Sounding on Apple TV is the same beta as everything else: me, in Amsterdam, mostly in the evenings. No support team, no promises. Tell me what breaks.

Full SoundCloud tracks need Go or Go+, because Sounding can’t play SoundCloud’s ads, and SoundCloud tracks on the decks need Go+ or DJ. Your own music needs nothing, not even an account.

The TV needs tvOS 27. Signing it in and linking work from iOS or iPadOS 26.5 or later, or macOS 27.
