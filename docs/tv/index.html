Sounding on the Apple TV.

Sounding started out as an Apple TV app, and the TV’s still where it’s loudest. It signs in by ear, it mixes on its own, and your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Watch are all the remote.

Invite only. I made this for me, and for a few people who now owe me a favour.

It signs in by ear.

  • No typing a password with the Siri Remote. Open Sounding on your iPhone near the TV and it offers to sign it in.
  • It finds the TV by its chime. Press Play Sound on the TV and it plays three notes, with a code riding underneath that’s too high for you to hear. Your iPhone listens for it, only while that screen’s open, and asks: Sign in to Sounding on this Apple TV?
  • Loud room, or microphone off? Scan the QR code on the TV with your iPhone camera instead. Or tap Use Without SoundCloud and just play your own music.
  • Phone in another room? When the TV asks, your iPhone gets a notification: Living Room wants to sign in. Tap it and that’s that.

Now Playing, from the sofa.

  • The background is the artwork. Blurred and drifting, so every track brings its own colours. The thin lines over it move with the music.
  • Laid out like a tape deck. What’s on at the top left, the next two tracks beside it, and the waveform right along the bottom, with the comments on it.
  • Long sets scrub like tape. Swipe on the remote’s touch surface, or press left and right to jump 15 seconds, then click to play from there. A mix shows its tracklist along the tape, and Identify fills the gaps.
  • Leave it alone and it goes ambient. Just the artwork, big, over the same background.

Your cue points are on it.

  • Set a cue on the Mac, see it on the TV. Hot cues and memory cues sit on the TV’s waveform in their colours. They sync through your own iCloud.
  • Jump to one. More, then Cue Points, lists them with their times. Pick one and it plays from there.
  • Fix one from the sofa. Track Map lets you add, move and rename cues with the remote.
  • Where cues come from is on the DJ page.

Auto Mix, on the big screen.

  • The TV mixes your queue. Turn on Auto Mix and it brings the next track in on the phrase. It only beat-matches when it’s measured where the bars are on both tracks, and cuts cleanly on the bar when it hasn’t.
  • You can watch it happen. While it mixes, the waveform turns into two decks: the move, the bar it’s on, both EQs and the crossfader. Mix Now and Extend are right there.
  • There’s a Decks tab too. A DJ show for the room: two big deck heads, both lanes and the mix, readable from across the room.
  • Pick how it plays. Wedding DJ, Auto Mix or Junkie, and an energy that follows the night unless you tell it otherwise.

Play On, and every remote you own.

  • Send it to the TV. On your iPhone, iPad or Mac, tap Play On and pick the Apple TV. It carries on from the same second.
  • Whatever’s in your hand is the remote. Now Playing, Up Next and the volume all work the TV now, and your iPhone, iPad, Mac and Watch all see the same queue.
  • Or your wrist. On the Watch, the Digital Crown turns the TV up and down. The Siri Remote still has the TV’s own volume.
  • Share a link to it. Share a SoundCloud link from any app on your iPhone and send it to the TV: Play Now, Play Next or Add to End.

Top Shelf.

  • Put Sounding in the top row and the shelf fills with whatever you were in the middle of: Continue Listening, with the time left, then Recently Played, Your Likes and Mixes For You.
  • Select one and it plays. No opening the app and finding it again.
  • The artwork lives on the TV, so it’s sharp at 4K and still there when the internet isn’t.

Lyrics, if you want them.

  • They come from LRCLIB, a free lyrics database, and they’re off until you say yes. Nothing goes to it before then.
  • On the TV they go full screen over the artwork, the line being sung in white and the rest fading away. Select a line to jump to it.
  • Mixes don’t get lyrics. A two-hour set would be a lot of guessing.

And the rest of it.

SharePlay. On a FaceTime call, More, then SharePlay, and everyone on the call listens along in sync on their own iPhone, iPad, Mac or Apple TV, each with their own SoundCloud sign-in. Auto Mix sits it out while it’s on.

The Siri Remote. Click to play or pause, swipe to scrub. Hold any track for Add to Playlist or Edit Start & End. Menu always goes back one step and never leaves you stuck.

Your lights. With HomeKit, Sounding colours the lights you pick from the artwork and sets their brightness to the music. Only the ones you pick.

Your own music. Albums on your Mac, iPhone or iPad play on the TV too, streamed from the device they’re on.

Speakers. From the TV’s Output, send it on to AirPlay or Bluetooth speakers, or a Sonos.

The small stuff. Behind the Track, timed comments, a Sleep Timer that fades the last 30 seconds, and downloads if you’re on Go or Go+.

Fair warning.

The TV app is getting a redesign as I write this, so some of it will look a bit different by the time you see it. It’ll still sign in by ear.

A free SoundCloud account gets previews. Full tracks and downloads need Go or Go+.
