Support

Bugs, questions, ideas, all welcome. It’s just me making Sounding, so there’s no support team, but I do read every message (eventually).

sounding@alex.forbes.red

What helps

Say what you were doing, what you expected and what actually happened. A screenshot or screen recording is worth a lot of words.

  • Which device and system version: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV or Mac.
  • What you did right before it went wrong.
  • Whether it happens every time.
  • The track or mix, if it’s only that one.

A track won’t play

Sounding can’t play SoundCloud’s ads, so on a free account you get SoundCloud’s previews, and full tracks need Go or Go+. Offline downloads need Go or Go+ too. SoundCloud tracks on the DJ decks need Go+ or DJ. Your own music plays whatever plan you’re on, or with no account at all.

The Apple TV won’t sign in

Open Sounding on your iPhone, iPad or Mac on the same Wi-Fi as the TV. A card should pop up with the TV’s name: tap Sign In, and keep the device in the room while the TV plays its sound. If it says it couldn’t hear the TV, turn the TV up and try again. Some soundbars cut off the very high bit the sound relies on, so if yours does, scan the QR code on the TV instead. No card at all? Scan the code, or press Play Sound on the TV.

The Apple TV is asking for a code

The first time a device links to a TV, the TV shows a six-digit code and asks whether to allow it, even on your own Apple ID. Check the same code is on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, then choose Allow on the TV. After that it reconnects on its own. To start over, go to Settings, Remotes & Devices on the TV and forget the device.

Your data

There are no Sounding servers or Sounding accounts, so there’s nothing for me to look up or delete for you. The privacy policy says what’s stored where, and how to get rid of it.
