Send TestFlight feedback

If you are testing the beta, feedback from TestFlight reaches me with your device, system version and build already attached.

  1. Open the TestFlight app and choose Sounding.
  2. Tap Send Beta Feedback.
  3. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
  4. Add a screenshot if it helps, then send.

You can also take a screenshot while Sounding is open. TestFlight offers to share it as feedback straight away. If the app crashes, TestFlight asks whether you would like to send the crash report.

What to include

  • The device you are using, such as iPhone, Apple TV or Mac.
  • What you did just before the problem.
  • Whether it happens every time.
  • The track or mix, if it only happens with one.
