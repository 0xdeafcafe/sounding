Send TestFlight feedback

If you’re testing the beta, feedback sent from TestFlight reaches me with your device, system version and build already attached.

  1. Open the TestFlight app and choose Sounding.
  2. Tap Send Beta Feedback.
  3. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened.
  4. Add a screenshot if it helps, then send.

You can also take a screenshot while Sounding is open, and TestFlight offers to send it as feedback straight away. If the app crashes, TestFlight asks whether you’d like to send the crash report.

What to include

  • The device you’re using, such as iPhone, Apple TV or Mac.
  • What you did just before the problem.
  • Whether it happens every time.
  • The track or mix, if it only happens with one.
