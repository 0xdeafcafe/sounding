Send it from TestFlight

This is the quickest way. Feedback from TestFlight lands with your device, system version and build already attached, which saves us both an email or two.

  1. Open TestFlight and pick Sounding.
  2. Tap Send Beta Feedback.
  3. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what actually happened.
  4. Add a screenshot if it helps, then send.

Take a screenshot while Sounding’s open and TestFlight offers to send it there and then. If the app crashes, it asks whether to send the crash report (please do).

What helps

  • Which device: iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV or Mac.
  • What you did right before it went wrong.
  • Whether it happens every time.
  • The track or mix, if it’s only that one.

A track won’t play

If SoundCloud only offers your account a preview of a track, Sounding won’t play it at all. Without SoundCloud Go+, that’s more tracks than you’d think. Offline downloads need Go+ as well.

The Apple TV won’t sign in

On your iPhone, go to Settings, Devices, and add the Apple TV, then keep the phone near the TV while it plays its chime. If the room’s loud or the microphone’s off, tap Use QR Code Instead.

Not on the beta yet?

Sounding’s heading for the App Store at some point. Until then, TestFlight it is.
