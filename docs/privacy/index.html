Privacy policy

Short version: Sounding doesn’t collect anything. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Sounding is an independent app made by Alexander Forbes-Reed. It isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by SoundCloud.

What Sounding collects

Nothing. There are no Sounding servers, no Sounding accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. I don’t get any data about you or how you use the app. The app only talks to SoundCloud and Apple.

Your SoundCloud account

You sign in on SoundCloud’s own page. Sounding keeps that sign-in in the Keychain on your device, and encrypted in your private iCloud so your other devices can use it. It only ever sends it to SoundCloud, to fetch your library and play your music. Likes, comments and follows go straight to SoundCloud as well, and SoundCloud’s privacy policy covers that data.

What stays on your devices

Downloads, Music Analysis results (tempo, key and genre), cue points, setlists and settings live on the device. Music Analysis runs on the device too. If you’re signed in to iCloud, these sync between your own devices through your private iCloud database. I can’t read it.

Adding an Apple TV, Mac or Apple Watch

When you add an Apple TV or Mac, your iPhone sends the sign-in straight to it over your local network, encrypted with a one-time key. It doesn’t go through any server. The chime an Apple TV plays only carries a short, single-use code, never your sign-in. Your Apple Watch gets the sign-in from your iPhone over Apple’s own watch connection.

Microphone, camera and local network

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, the microphone listens for your Apple TV’s sign-in chime, and only while that screen is open. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. The camera is only used if you’d rather scan the code on your Apple TV. Local network access is used to find your own devices and nearby Polyphony sessions. None of it is stored or shared.

Polyphony

When you join a Polyphony session, the devices on your network share what’s playing and where it’s up to, so they stay in sync. Each speaker plays the track from SoundCloud with its own sign-in.

Song identification

Sounding identifies tracks inside long mixes with Apple’s ShazamKit. It makes a fingerprint from the track’s own audio (not from your microphone) and Apple matches it, so Apple’s privacy policy covers that part.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings to remove the sign-in from your devices. Delete the app to remove everything it stored on that device. To delete Sounding’s iCloud data, go to Settings, Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Storage.
