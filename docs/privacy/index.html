Privacy policy

Short version: Sounding doesn’t collect anything. Last updated 6 November 2026.

Sounding is an independent app made by Alexander Forbes-Reed. It isn’t affiliated with SoundCloud.

What Sounding collects

Nothing. There are no Sounding servers, no Sounding accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. I don’t get any data about you or how you use the app. The app only talks to SoundCloud and Apple.

Your SoundCloud account

You sign in on SoundCloud’s own page. Sounding keeps that sign-in in the Keychain on your device and only ever sends it to SoundCloud, to fetch your library and play your music. Likes, comments and follows go straight to SoundCloud as well, and SoundCloud’s privacy policy covers that data.

What stays on your devices

Downloads, analysis results, cue points, setlists and settings live on the device. If you’re signed in to iCloud, they sync between your own devices through your private iCloud container. I can’t read it.

Moving your sign-in between devices

When you link an Apple TV, Mac or Apple Watch, your iPhone sends the sign-in straight to it over your local network, signed with a one-time secret. It doesn’t go through any server.

Camera and local network

On iPhone, the camera is only used to scan the code on your Apple TV. Local network access is only used to find your own devices. Neither is stored or shared.

Song identification

Sounding identifies tracks inside long mixes with Apple’s ShazamKit, so Apple’s privacy policy covers that part.

Deleting your data

Sign out in Settings to remove the sign-in from your devices. Delete the app to remove everything it stored on that device. To delete Sounding’s iCloud data, go to Settings, Apple Account, iCloud, Manage Storage.
